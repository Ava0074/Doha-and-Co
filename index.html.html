Our Philosophy

Where craft meets culture.

Doha & Co. was born from a simple conviction: that specialty coffee in Qatar should look as extraordinary as it tastes. The iconic blush pink cup with navy branding isn't just packaging — it's a statement about what modern luxury means in this city.

We source exclusively specialty-grade beans, working with ethical farms across the world's premier coffee-growing regions. Every roast is calibrated for the drinks we craft — not generic espresso, but living flavor profiles shaped by altitude, harvest, and intention.

This is coffee designed to be shared, photographed, and savored. A ritual for the people who move Doha forward.
